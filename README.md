# 🛒 E-commerce Trend Analysis - SQL

## 📌 Aim

The **E-commerce Trend Analysis** project focuses on analyzing e-commerce business data using Structured Query Language (SQL) to generate meaningful insights into customer behavior, product performance, sales trends, and operational efficiency.

The project explores business questions related to popular products, top customers, revenue generation, purchasing frequency, product reviews, inventory levels, discounts, shipping timelines, and order statuses.

The primary goal is to demonstrate how SQL can be used to analyze business data and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Identify popular products and top-performing customers.
- Analyze revenue trends and customer spending patterns.
- Understand purchase frequency and customer behavior.
- Evaluate product performance using sales, reviews, and stock levels.
- Analyze order statuses and shipment timelines.
- Examine discount usage and its potential business impact.
- Identify low-stock products and unsold inventory.
- Generate actionable insights to support business decisions.

## 📊 Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Sales and Revenue Analysis

Analyze sales performance and revenue trends to understand business performance and identify popular products.

### 2. Customer Analysis

Explore customer spending habits, purchase frequency, and top customers to understand purchasing behavior.

### 3. Product Performance Analysis

Evaluate product performance using sales, customer reviews, and stock availability.

### 4. Inventory Management

Identify products with low stock levels and unsold inventory to support inventory planning and replenishment decisions.

### 5. Discount Analysis

Examine discount usage and explore how discount strategies relate to sales performance and revenue.

### 6. Order and Shipment Analysis

Analyze order statuses and shipment timelines to understand order fulfillment and operational performance.

## 🔍 Business Questions

This project aims to answer questions such as:

- Which products are the most popular?
- Who are the top customers based on spending?
- How does revenue vary across products or time periods?
- How frequently do customers make purchases?
- Which products perform well based on sales and reviews?
- Which products may require inventory replenishment?
- Which products have remained unsold?
- How are discounts used across orders?
- What do order statuses reveal about order fulfillment?
- How long do shipments take to reach customers?

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis can help e-commerce businesses:
